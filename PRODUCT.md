# Product

## Register

product

## Users
University students using phones and laptops between classes, at home, and during exam preparation. Their primary job is to find the right notes, previous papers, syllabus, and study guidance quickly without learning a complicated interface.

## Product Purpose
UNIO gives students one dependable place for course resources, profile-aware subject discovery, PDF study material, and lightweight AI assistance. Success means a student can orient themselves, find a relevant resource, and begin studying within seconds.

## Brand Personality
Intelligent, human, refined. Calm enough for long study sessions, warm enough to feel supportive, and precise enough to earn trust.

## Anti-references
Do not resemble a neon gaming dashboard, generic purple-gradient AI SaaS, crowded university portal, glass-card showcase, or decorative concept that hides ordinary controls. Do not make unfinished features look functional.

## Design Principles
1. Put the next useful study action first.
2. Make academic structure feel simple and familiar.
3. Use motion to preserve context and confirm state, never to delay work.
4. Earn premium quality through typography, rhythm, and states rather than decoration.
5. Keep every visible action honest, understandable, and operational.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard-only navigation, visible focus, semantic controls, touch targets of at least 44px, color-independent state communication, readable contrast, zoom, and a reduced-motion mode that removes smooth scrolling and decorative choreography.
